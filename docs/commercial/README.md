# Astor AI Commercial Pack

This folder contains public-facing and business-facing materials for Astor AI, Astor Butler, and Astor Concierge.

## Documents

- `BRAND_GUIDE_RU.md` - narrative guide for restaurants, staff and partners.
- `COMMERCIAL_OFFER_RU.md` - commercial offer for Astor AI, Astor Butler, and Astor Concierge.
- `AERIS_SERVICE_AGREEMENT_DRAFT_RU.md` - договор оказания услуг для AERIS: 60 000 рублей за запуск и 20 000 рублей в месяц за сопровождение после бесплатного тестового месяца.
- `OOO_SCHASTYE_AERIS_PROD_BUDGET_RU.md` - Приложение N 1 для ООО «Счастье» / AERIS: смета запуска, бесплатный первый тестовый месяц, НДС, инфраструктура и экономика YandexGPT.
- `OOO_SCHASTYE_AERIS_PRINT_PACKAGE_RU.md` - памятка к печати и подписи: какие документы печатать, что заполнить и как объяснить условия Тариэлю.
- `VIDEO_SHOOTING_DAY_TZ_RU.md` - one-day shooting brief for restaurateur presentations.
- `BENCHMARK_COMPARISON_RU.md` - comparison against a simple Telegram bot and enterprise AI platforms.
- `INFRA_DNS_NOTES_RU.md` - VCG/DNS/hosting notes for the first commercial deployment.
- `AI_AGENT_BUSINESS_PLAN_RU.md` - strategy and business plan for the AI-agent direction.

## Status

These materials are commercial drafts, not signed legal or financial documents.
Before external use, verify:

- party legal names and bank details;
- final service scope and SLA;
- tax treatment and invoicing model;
- hosting provider invoices and pass-through expenses;
- public claims and benchmark methodology.
